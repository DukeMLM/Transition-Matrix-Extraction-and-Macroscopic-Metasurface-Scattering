## Summary

`dT glob` and `dT block` are the PREDICTED relative T-recovery errors at the frequency-matched measured sigma, each given as **systematic / iid**.  The whitening level is a MODEL-vs-CST discrepancy, not iid noise (`results/REAL_RETRIEVAL.md` par. 4.3), so the iid figure -- which averages the discrepancy down over all modal entries -- is a lower bound only, while the systematic figure assumes the discrepancy aligns with the worst-conditioned direction of H+.  The truth depends on the discrepancy's STRUCTURE, which is an M3 measurement: **no Gate E verdict is claimed from either end**.  Blocks are invariant multipole blocks (l, pol) x (l', pol'), not coordinates of the symmetry basis.  `sigma need` is the accuracy required to reach the 5% global target under the systematic model.
